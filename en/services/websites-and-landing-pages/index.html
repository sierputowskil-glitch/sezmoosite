FAQ – common questions

Which technologies do you use?

Most often WordPress and React, but we choose the technology per project: interactivity, speed, ease of editing and planned integrations all matter. You get our recommendation with the quote.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Every site comes with a CMS, and after launch we show you how to use it.

What does the build process look like?

We build the site on a staging server, test it across devices and browsers, then share it for approval. After sign-off we move it to your hosting, connect the domain and set up redirects from old URLs.

Do you design the site too?

Yes – design is part of our UX/UI design service. One team from design to code means fewer handovers and fewer misunderstandings.

What if a bug shows up after launch?

We fix it. Changes beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately before we start.
