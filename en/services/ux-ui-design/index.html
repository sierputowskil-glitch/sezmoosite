Overview

Good design is not pretty screens – it is the right decisions.

What the user sees first, where they click and what they do next decides whether a site sells or just looks nice. That is why we start with the business goal and the people who will actually use the product.

From there we build the structure, wireframes and a visual layer consistent with the brand. Every project is tested on a clickable prototype before it reaches developers – so a change costs hours, not weeks of rework.

01

UX research & workshops

We start by talking through goals, audiences and constraints. We look at competitors, the data available and the paths users take today to reach their goal – or where they get lost.

The result is a shared picture of what the product should do and for whom, before the first screen is drawn.

02

Information architecture & wireframes

We organise content and features into a structure that makes sense to the user, not to the company org chart.

Wireframes show the layout of key views without colour or imagery, which keeps the conversation on what matters: the order of information and the path to contact.

UI design

We give the interface the character of the brand. Typography, colour, icons and components are designed view by view – for desktop and mobile.

Photo, video and graphics are produced by the same team, so the design never waits on material and looks consistent from day one.

FAQ – common questions

What is the difference between UX and UI?

UX is the logic of the product: structure, flows and how easily people reach their goal. UI is the layer you see – typography, colour and components. We design both, because one does not work without the other.

I already have a website. Can it be improved without starting over?

Yes. We start with a UX audit, show where users get lost and design changes in order of impact.

Which tools do you use?

Figma and the full Adobe Creative Cloud suite – we pick the tool for the task, not the other way round. You get access to the files, the prototype and the design system, with no locked formats.

Will you build the site you designed?

Yes. Development is done in-house – from WordPress, through React, to any technology the project needs. See our Websites service.

How long does a UX/UI project take?

It depends on scope – a landing page is a different scale from a store or an app. We give a timeline and a quote after the brief or a short workshop.
