Frequently asked questions.

How much does a marketing retainer cost?

The figure is set individually – it depends on how much material you need each month and of what kind. After a short call you get one monthly price and a list of what it includes. We do not sell off-the-shelf packages, because every brand publishes at a different pace.

What if I need something different this month?

The hours are not tied to one service. One month it can be a shoot day and an edit, the next a product session, a catalogue and a campaign. We agree the scope in the rolling plan.

Is a retainer a long-term contract?

A retainer works when material is produced in a rhythm and the team knows the brand, so we usually agree on a longer engagement. The minimum term and notice period are set individually with the quote.

Can you take over the whole marketing?

Yes. If you have no marketing department, we run the whole thing: planning, production and publishing. If you have a marketing manager, we take on production and creative while they keep strategy and decisions.

How do I know what the hours were spent on?

Every month ends with a breakdown: the list of material produced and the hours behind each item, sent with the invoice. During the month you can follow progress in ClickUp, and you approve priorities before production – nothing happens without your knowledge.

Do you work outside the Tricity?

Yes. We are based in Gdańsk and shoot across Poland and abroad. Shoot days are planned so one trip delivers several weeks of publishing – travel does not eat into your package.
