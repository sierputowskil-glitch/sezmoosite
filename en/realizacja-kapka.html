[ 01 ] · ABOUT THE PROJECT

A brand built
around a single drop.

Kapka Cafe is a small, intimate coffee shop that needed a brand to match its scale: warm, friendly and recognisable from across the street. We started from the name, “kapka”, meaning a drop, and made it the heart of the whole identity.

From one idea we built a full system: a mark with a to-go cup held by a hand, a coffee-drop mascot with real character, and a consistent palette and type carried across everything, from the menu to the in-store signage.

[ 02 ] · THE MARK

A logo that instantly
says “coffee to go”.

[ 03 ] · IDENTITY SYSTEM

From the mark
to the whole venue.

B1, 01

Mark & wordmark

A cup-and-hand symbol plus a soft, hand-drawn “Kapka Cafe” wordmark, legible on the sign, in the app and on the cup.

B1, 02

Drop mascot

A smiling coffee drop with a cup and a sandwich, the brand character used across social media, banners and as a window sticker in the venue.

B1, 03

Palette & type

Warm roasted-coffee browns, a cream base and a soft, rounded typeface that build a friendly café character and keep everything in one tone.

B1, 04

Materials & venue

Menu, Facebook banner, sign, dimensional letters on the counter and stickers, the identity carried onto real guest touchpoints.

“One simple idea, a drop, gave Kapka Cafe a mark, a mascot and a coherent language that looks just as good on a cup, in the window and across social media.”
— SEZMOO team · Branding & identity
