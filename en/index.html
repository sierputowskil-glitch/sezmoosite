Idea, production

SEZMOO is a creative agency with 14 years of marketing experience, based in Gdańsk. We bring every key area together in one place: from photo and video production, through graphic design and web design, to online marketing (social media and paid campaigns) and offline with our own production. We run projects end to end, from idea and creative to finished materials rolled out in digital and in the real world. We work project by project, and for brands that need ongoing production, on a retainer model too.
