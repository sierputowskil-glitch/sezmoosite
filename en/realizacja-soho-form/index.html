ABOUT THE PROJECT

Craft that
looks premium.

Soho Form is a family business with a long tradition. It makes sofas, armchairs, beds and joinery to order – for commercial and private interiors. The furniture had long been on par with premium brands, but the communication did not show it yet.

We designed the brand from scratch: the mark, a brand manual with palette and typography, and then a website that presents the offer and projects the way architects and investors expect.
